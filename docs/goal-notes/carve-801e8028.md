### Byte-exactness, measured against the linked DOL (not just objdiff)

`tools/carve_diff.sh` **cannot align a multi-function unit** - it diffs retail's range against our
object from instruction 0, so on this 2-function object it reported 11 and 16 "differing
instructions" and `NOT byte-exact`. It does the same on the already-flip-verified
`Carve801E7C14` (32 "differing" on an 8-instruction range), so that verdict is the tool
mis-aligning, not a defect in this carve. Byte-exactness established instead two ways:

1. Per function, our object's instructions against retail's, with only the `bl` field
   differing: `48 00 00 15` in retail vs `48 00 00 01` in our relocatable `.o` at instruction
   +3 of `fn_801E8028` and +5 of `fn_801E8048`. That is the expected **unrelocated
   R_PPC_REL24 placeholder** in an unlinked object, and it is the same single field on both
   functions and the only difference in either.
2. In the **linked** `build/G2ME01/main.dol`, with our object in the link, `dol_read.py` reads
   back retail's exact bytes at both addresses - `0x801E8028`: `94 21 ff f0 7c 08 02 a6 90 01 00
   14 **48 00 00 15** 80 01 00 14 7c 08 03 a6 38 21 00 10 4e 80 00 20`, and `0x801E8048`:
   `... 28 03 00 00 90 01 00 14 41 82 00 08 **48 00 00 15** 80 01 00 14 ...`. mwldeppc filled the
   relocation with retail's value, and the sha1 holds.
