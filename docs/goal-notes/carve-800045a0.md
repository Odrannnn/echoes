## Spellings: `fn_800045F4` is the only one that took iterations

Three of the four were byte-exact on the first compile. The vector destructor was not, and the
reason is worth keeping: **the four dead stores at `r1+0x14/0x08/0x10/0x0C` are the inliner's
by-value parameter home slots, and they only appear when the `destroy(begin, end)` chain is
`inline` and its loop walks plain pointers.**

| spelling | result |
| --- | --- |
| plain `T* it = mItems; while (it != end) ++it;` (no iterator struct) | frame `0x10` not `0x20`, 4 stores absent - 116 bytes vs retail's 132 (16 short) |
| `static void DestroyImpl(struct SIt, struct SIt)` (not inlined) | the whole loop outlined into `DestroyImpl` and the params passed by address: `addi r3,r1,8` / `addi r4,r1,12` - wrong (v1 of this run) |

| `static inline`, and the loop body destructured to `T* cur = begin.current; T* last = end.current;` | **byte-exact**: `mr r4,r3`, `add r0,r3,r0`, and the four dead stores, in retail's order |
