## Operational trap worth more than the wall it cost

`goal_check.sh` honours `MP_GOAL_TREE` / `MP_GOAL_BASE` / `MP_GOAL_JUDGE`, and the shell handed to
a lane session in this worktree has them exported pointing at **another lane** (here
`.../wt-mp2-goal-L13`), even though the prompt says to work in this worktree. A bare
`./tools/goal_check.sh build/goal/item.json` therefore `cd`s to that other tree and judges *its*
`build/goal/item.json` — the run reports `item <someone else's id>`, `FAIL gate.sh`, and writes
its logs into the other tree. Two runs were wasted on that. The fix is to unset all four
(`env -u MP_GOAL_TREE -u MP_GOAL_BASE -u MP_GOAL_JUDGE -u MP_GOAL_LANE ./tools/goal_check.sh
build/goal/item.json`) or set them to this worktree's absolute paths; the PASS above is from the
unset form. The lane driver's own `build/goal/check-*.log` in this worktree are from *other* items
(`carve-800e10ec`, `carve-801fa780`) and were stale for this item until the two runs recorded here.
