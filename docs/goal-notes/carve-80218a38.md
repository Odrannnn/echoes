`NOT byte-exact` for the *linked-and-`Matching`* twin as well:


2026-09-28 is the evidence that the difference is the tool's, not the code's. Byte-exactness here
is established by `flip_test.sh` (`PASS`) and the unchanged DOL sha1, as the item's own rule says.

name.** `./tools/check_decl_order.py --unit MetroidPrime/ScriptLoader/Carve80218A38.c` printed
`ok: 0 unit(s) checked, none emits its functions out of retail order` — a vacuous pass on a
misspelled argument. The report's name is `main/MetroidPrime/ScriptLoader/Carve80218A38`, and with
that spelling it prints `ok: 1 unit(s) checked`. A one-function carve cannot get the order wrong
either way, but a green line from this tool is worth the spelling.
