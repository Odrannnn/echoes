## Why it is byte-exact, and on what evidence

The claim is exactly the 8 bytes named and nothing else, so the file emits one `lfs f1, 0x8(r3)`
and one `blr` and nothing can move.

- **The receiver is a `CScanTree`, and that is measured, not assumed.** Both callers pass
  `this + 0x1B8` of a `CPauseScreen`:
  - `UpdateHistoryColors` (0x80202064): `mr r15, r3` at 0x80202088, then
    `addi r3, r15, 0x1b8 / bl fn_80210978 / fmr f30, f1` at 0x802020FC..0x80202104.
  - `CPauseScreen::Draw` (0x80205CCC): `mr r29, r3` at 0x80205CF8, then
    `addi r3, r29, 0x1b8 / bl fn_80210978 / fmr f29, f1` at 0x80205F24..0x80205F2C.
  Retail names the member at +0x1B8 itself: `CPauseScreen`'s constructor does
  `addi r3, r28, 0x1b8` (0x8020C7DC), leaves `r3` alone across the eight stores in between, and
  then `bl __ct__9CScanTreeFv` (0x8020C824). So `+0x1B8` is `CScanTree mScanTree`.
- **The word at +8 is `CScanTree`'s first float member.** Retail's `CScanTree::CScanTree`
  (0x80211C3C, 0x80 bytes) stores `-1` at `+0` and `+4` (0x80211C64, 0x80211C6C) and
  `lbl_8041D8BC` - `symbols.txt:24407`, typed `data:float` - at `+8` (0x80211C70). That is the
  layout `include/MetroidPrime/CScanTree.hpp` declares as `int mSelectedNode; int
  mPreviousNode; float mTransition;` at `CHECK_SIZEOF(CScanTree, 0x40)`.
- **The shape has a byte-shape twin in a `Matching` unit**:

  Same two instructions, differing only in the displacement. That is evidence for the shape; the
  result is `flip_test`, not the twin.

## Why it had to be its own unit, and why it links

- The dtk range it comes out of, `auto_03_8020EE18_text` (0x8020EE18..0x80210980, 18 functions),
  is otherwise unclaimed, and a unit may not claim two discontiguous ranges in one section
  (`dtk dol split` fails with "Cyclic dependency ... link order").
- `0x80210980` is the start of the landed `Carve80210980.c`, so this claim cannot be folded into
  it without a split of an existing `Matching` unit. Proximate carves link; a carve that *starts*
  where another unit's `.text` ends does not (`docs/RUNNING_THE_DECOMP.md`, "The carve vein"), and
  the two are 8 bytes apart here.
- The lower neighbour `fn_80210968` (0x80210968, 0x10, `cntlzw`/`srwi` over the word at `+0x24`)
  is not part of the claim and is not trivial.
- The directory is retail's own, from the nearest claimed range: 0x1D88 bytes past the end of
  `MetroidPrime/ScriptObjects/CScanTreeInventory.cpp` (0x8020EBF0..0x8020EE18).
- No `TARGET_PC` arm is needed: nothing in `src/` or `include/` names `fn_80210978` by symbol,
  and both callers are inside `MetroidPrime/CPauseScreen.cpp`, which is `Object(NonMatching, ...)`
  and is built from our source only on the DOL build. There is no duplicate in
  `src/MetroidPrime/PortLinkStubs.cpp` (`grep -c fn_80210978` = 0).
