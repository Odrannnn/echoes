## What the function is

`stw r3, gLoader_Kralee@sda21(r0)` / `blr` — Kralee's loader setter. The byte-shape twin of
the matched `fn_80200E3C` (`src/MetroidPrime/ScriptLoader/Carve80200E3C.c`). The two differ
in exactly the 16-bit `@sda21` displacement, because their slots are 8 bytes apart, and
Parasite's own unclaimed `fn_80200EFC` is the same shape a third time:

| function | slot | bytes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fn_80200E3C` | `gLoader_SpacePirate` 0x80419358 | `90 6D 95 D8` / `4E 80 00 20` |
| `fn_80200E70` (this carve) | `gLoader_Kralee` 0x80419360 | `90 6D 95 E0` / `4E 80 00 20` |
| `fn_80200EFC` (still auto) | `gLoader_Parasite` 0x80419368 | `90 6D 95 E8` / `4E 80 00 20` |

Measured off `build/G2ME01/main.elf` after the build, not recalled.

**Argument, read off the callers.** `grep -rl fn_80200E70 build/G2ME01/` finds exactly one
REL module importing it, Kralee (module 37) — `Kralee.plf`/`Kralee.preplf` are its import
lists — and two call sites, both in `build/G2ME01/Kralee/asm/auto_00_0000009C_text.s`:

* `RELExit` (0x24 bytes) does `li r3, 0` then `bl fn_80200E70` — teardown.
* `fn_37_10C` (0x30 bytes, reached from `RELMain`) does `lis r4, fn_37_13C@ha` ,
  `addi r0, r4, fn_37_13C@l` , `lis r3, lbl_37_bss_8@ha` , `stwu r0, lbl_37_bss_8@l(r3)` and
  `bl fn_80200E70` with `r3` still `&lbl_37_bss_8`.

So the argument is that record's address. **The record is 4 bytes**, not a struct:
`build/G2ME01/Kralee/asm/auto_05_00000000_bss.s` gives `lbl_37_bss_8 size:0x4` and the
module's entire `.bss` is 0xC. That is the difference from module 72, whose `lbl_72_bss_24`
record is 0x1C and is why `Carve80200E3C.c` types its argument as a 0x1C struct; Kralee loads
one entity, so its record is the bare `FScriptLoader` and the argument type here is a pointer
to a one-word struct. The unit takes `gLoader_Kralee` as `extern` and claims `.text` only —
`Kralee.cpp` already claims and defines the `.sbss 0x80419360..0x80419368` slot.

## Two tool caveats worth knowing (negative results)

1. **`tools/carve_diff.sh` reports `NOT byte-exact` for this carve, and that is not a defect.**
   ```
   $ python3 tools/carve_diff.sh 80200E70 8 build/G2ME01/src/MetroidPrime/ScriptLoader/Carve80200E70.o
   retail: 2 instructions, 8 bytes
   ours  : 2 instructions, 8 bytes
     +0   retail: 80200e70 stw r3,-27168(r13)  ours: 00000000 stw r3,0(0)
   differing instructions: 1
   NOT byte-exact
   ```
   The whole shape of this family is one `sda21` store, so an unlinked object's only
   instruction is a relocation placeholder (`0(0)`) and it can never compare equal against a
   linked address. Reading our side out of `build/G2ME01/main.elf` instead gives
   `90 6d 95 e0 / 4e 80 00 20`, retail's bytes. For this family `carve_diff.sh` is only
   informative once the object is linked; `flip_test.sh` is the decision.
2. **`check_decl_order.py --unit <mine>` reporting "0 unit(s) checked" is expected, not a
   miss.** `--unit` filters the work list in `docs/research/decl_order.md`
   (`check_decl_order.py:143`), and a brand-new unit is not on it. It is not on it because
   nothing is wrong with it: a one-function unit cannot be permuted, and `flip_test` passing
   is the by-construction proof the tool's docstring wants.
