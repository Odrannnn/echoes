Both twins are exact, `bl` displacement apart:

- `fn_80241C90` is `__dt__5CMainFv` (0x800087DC, 0x3C) - `CMain::~CMain() {}` at
  `src/MetroidPrime/main.cpp:517` - word for word, `bl Free__7CMemoryFPCv` included.  The flag is
  re-tested as a **short** (`extsh. r0,r4`, not `extsb`), and `if (flag > 0)` sits **inside**
  `if (self)`, so the receiver's `beq` lands on the epilogue.  The epilogue returns the receiver
  in r3 (`mr r3,r31`), which is what fixes the return as `void*`: spelled `void`, mwcceppc drops
  the move and the body is 14 instructions / 56 bytes, four bytes short of even the claim and
  twelve short of retail's 0x3C.
- `fn_80241CCC` is `DisableFog__Q29CGameArea8CAreaFogFv` (0x80056DE8, 0xC) -
  `mFogMode = kRFM_None;` at `src/MetroidPrime/CGameArea.cpp:1592` - exactly the same three
  instructions.  With twelve call sites that shape is a **default constructor** more plausibly
  than a clear, and every caller materialises the object in place and then writes it, so nothing
  observable decides it.  The twin's member is `ERglFogMode` (`CGameArea.hpp:120`); a constant
  store needs no type, so the parameter is spelled `int*`, and the twin note says the same.

**All thirteen pass `li r4,-1` immediately before the call**, so `extsh. r0,r4` is -1, `ble` is
always taken and `Free__7CMemoryFPCv` is unreachable from every call site in retail.  `-1` is
MWCC's "destroy, do not free me afterwards", so retail uses these as the member's destructor walk;
the free is live in the *source*, which is why the branch is written and why the bytes are right.
`fn_80241CCC` has twelve call sites (eleven in `auto_*` units, one ours at 0x80220478 on
`this + 4`).
