## The one thing that was not obvious: the `.sbss` has to be claimed, and the judge says so

My first attempt declared **both** slots `extern` and claimed `.text` only — the shape
`Carve80200E3C.c` uses, because there `SpacePirate.cpp` owns the slot. Here nothing owned
`lbl_804195C8`: it was still dtk's `auto_10_804195C8_sbss`. The DOL still built and
`flip_test` still passed, but the judge failed:

```
goal_check: FAIL carve-8022a570 - 1 failing check(s): gate.sh
  FAIL  gate.sh
        GATE FAIL: link-gap
  link gap not accounted for:
    gap grew: lbl_804195C8 is not in port_link_gap_list.md
```

This is the `link_gap.py` half of the gate, and it is the only instrument that sees it: a
DOL that links fine and an `extern` a host build cannot resolve are the same tree. The fix is
the one every other unit in this family already makes — `SpacePirate.cpp`, `Kralee.cpp`,
`Parasite.cpp` and the rest all claim `.text` **and** their own `.sbss` slot in one unit.
Claiming `.sbss 0x804195C8 end:0x804195D0` and defining it as the same two-word `SLoaderSlot`
as its neighbour retires `auto_10_804195C8_sbss` instead of leaving a second definition of the
same eight bytes, and the gap stops growing.

The 8 bytes are right: `auto_10_804195C8_sbss.s` is `.balign 8`, `.skip 0x8` — byte for byte
the shape of `gLoader_EmperorIngStage2Tentacle` next to it.

**Lesson worth keeping: a carve of a setter is not just the `.text` range.** If the slot it
stores into is not already claimed by a neighbouring unit, the carve must claim it or
`link-gap` fails on a change that builds, flips and hashes. The signal is `link-gap`, not the
DOL.

## Notes for the next lane

- The `EmperorIngStage2Tentacle.cpp` and `EmperorIngStage3.cpp` headers both say their setters
  are "deliberately NOT claimed ... must stay in dtk's auto unit". That is half the requirement
  and worth correcting in place when someone next edits them: the reason is that REL modules
  import these **by retail name**, so they cannot be *renamed* — not that they cannot be
  *supplied*. Reproducing `fn_<addr>` verbatim in a `.c` satisfies the name constraint and
  puts our bytes in the link, which is exactly what `Carve80200E3C.c` and now this unit do for
  modules 72, EmperorIngStage2Tentacle and SwarmBasics.
