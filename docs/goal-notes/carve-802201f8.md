## The one unmeasured part in the reason, now measured

The reason said "the only unmeasured part is the C spelling of that doubled `addic.` test". It is
`self->mHolder.~SHolderAt180()` on a struct whose `mName` is an `rstl::string`, and it compiles
to retail's bytes on the first try - no spelling loop was needed. The mechanism is the one
`src/MetroidPrime/main.cpp:288-293` documents for `SNodeKey`: mwcceppc emits a null test on the
address of every class-type member it destroys, and `~rstl::string` is `{ internal_dereference(); }`
(`include/rstl/string.hpp:193`), so the wrapper's test and the string's test are the pair, and the
`bl` is retail's out-of-line
`internal_dereference__Q24rstl66basic_string<c,Q24rstl14char_traits<c>,Q24rstl17rmemory_allocator>Fv`
at 0x802FE9B8 (`symbols.txt:13842`).

## `.cpp`, not `.c` - and why that is forced

`fn_802201F8` is a dtk placeholder name (`fn_<addr>`), so the definition must keep that name
unmangled or objdiff pairs nothing. `interna**l_dereference__Q24rstl66basic_string<c,...>Fv**` is
**not** made of identifier characters, so a `.c` cannot declare it at all - the same measurement
`src/MetroidPrime/Carve800E10EC.cpp:22-31` records for the `<w,...>` variant. Hence `.cpp` with
`extern "C"`, exactly as `Carve800E10EC.cpp` does. `extern "C"` is also required on the
declarations of `Free__7CMemoryFPCv` and `__dt__20SLdrEditorPropertiesFv`: those identifiers *are*
mwcceppc's mangled names, and a C++-linkage declaration would mangle them a second time.
