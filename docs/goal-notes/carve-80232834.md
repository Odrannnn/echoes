   `CFogOverlayRel.cpp` is the module's reimplementation and its header comment is the place

   Rule of thumb that held here: after the four files, `grep -rn "auto_03_80232834_text"
   src/` - exactly one hit, and it was inside a comment making a claim the carve had falsified.
   Prefer fixing that one sentence over filing a `NEW:`; a doc fix is not queueable work and
   this one is not even a doc, it is the unit's own justification.
